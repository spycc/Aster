Privacy

Who we are

Aster Cargo B.V. is an international logistics company in the Netherlands. This notice explains what happens to the details you send through the enquiry form on this website. Questions about it can be sent to sales.eu@aster-cargo.com.

What the form collects

The form asks for your company name, the contact person, and an email address. You may also give a telephone number, the services you need, cargo details, transport requirements, and any other note. Only the company name, contact person, and email address have to be filled in.

Why we use it

We use what you send so we can read the enquiry and reply about freight and logistics. If the conversation continues into a shipment or another piece of work, we use the same details for that work. We do not use them to build an advertising list, and we do not sell them.

Who receives it

When you submit the form, the details are delivered to sales.eu@aster-cargo.com. The delivery is handled by FormSubmit, a form service. This website does not keep its own copy of the form.

How long we keep it

We keep an enquiry for as long as we need it to answer you. If a shipment or a business relationship follows, we keep what we need for that work. You can ask us to delete an enquiry by emailing sales.eu@aster-cargo.com.

What you can ask

You can ask what we hold about you, ask for a correction, or ask us to delete it, at the same address. If you are in the European Economic Area and you are not satisfied with the reply, you can complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority, Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Cookies

This website does not set cookies, and it does not use analytics or advertising tools.

A cookie is a small piece of information a site can ask your browser to store, so the site can recognise you later or count visits. We do not do that here. The pages show information and take an enquiry. Opening them does not place a cookie from Aster Cargo.

Your browser may still keep ordinary technical information it needs in order to display the page. That is not a cookie set by this website. If we add a cookie later, this page will name it, say what it is for, and say how you can refuse it.
